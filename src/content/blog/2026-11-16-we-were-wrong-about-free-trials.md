---
title: "We were wrong about our free trials"
description: "Why we stopped giving a free trial on signup."
pubDate: 'November 16 2026'
---

A couple of weeks ago we tried something for [OpenStatus](https://www.openstatus.dev). Every new user was put on a 2-week free trial.

We thought they would see the full value of OpenStatus. It was wrong.

![New customers vs active subscribers](/assets/free-trials-experiment.png)

They could do everything without hitting a wall.

What we changed is we now start a 14-day trial only when a user hits a limit and wants to go further.

We can now clearly identify users with buying intent, instead of just hobbyists signing up.
